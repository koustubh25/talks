Koustubh flags em dashes (—) and dramatic em-dash-then-full-stop rhythm as reading "AI generated." In slide copy and written deliverables, avoid the em dash entirely; use commas or plain sentences instead. Also avoid stacked short fragments for effect.

**Why:** it's a visible tell that undermines a polished, human-authored feel for his conference deck.

**How to apply:** write flowing natural sentences; prefer commas/colons or a clean full stop over "X — Y." Applies to the [[project_experts_live_talk]] concept deck (demo/talk/slides/concept.html) and any other prose I produce for him. When editing, grep for "—" before publishing.
