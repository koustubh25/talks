## What Dev Days is

- Global, in-person, user-group / community-led events. Free. Open to developers of all levels.
- Organizers: community leaders, GitHub Stars, MVPs, other developers. Must be approved via the request form (gh.io/dev-days-2026).
- Promoted on the official Dev Days page and Microsoft/GitHub social channels.
- Registration only through Luma. Event naming: `Dev Days [City Name]`.

## Formats

1. **Sessions-only:** technical talks and demos.
2. **Sessions + Workshop:** talks plus a hands-on lab (recommended). Current organizer guide says **one session + one workshop**.

FAQ rule: use the core slide deck (Copilot app or CLI) and include **at least one hands-on workshop**.

## Organizer guide highlights

- 15-minute setup buffer at start
- Min. recommended capacity: 30
- Venue: computer lab or laptop space with power, tables, stable Wi-Fi, projector, mics for big rooms
- Recruit speakers, plus facilitators/proctors for the lab; share content in advance; have a backup speaker plan
- Sponsors can cover food; program reimburses F&B up to USD $400/event (pre-approved, receipts)
- Reminder cadence example: Wednesday reminder + Friday follow-up for a Saturday event
- Post-event: attendee survey + mandatory organizer survey
- Official graphics generator: https://gh.io/dev-day/assets
- Approved organizers get a Slack channel; hosts may get a Copilot Max coupon and swag

## Event Code of Conduct (summary)

Applies to attendees, speakers, organizers, sponsors. Expect respect and inclusive language. Prohibited: harassment, sexualized content, trolling, personal/political attacks, **sustained disruption of talks**, and **photographing/recording people without consent** (relevant if you record your talk or the audience). Non-urgent reports: events@github.com.

## Design/brand intent (PRODUCT.md)

Personality: "energetic, technical, and welcoming." Avoid stiff corporate tone. Accessible to newcomers but credible to experienced developers.
